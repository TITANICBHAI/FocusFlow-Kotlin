---
name: Settings help and text-size destinations
description: Product structure for text-size controls and Settings help content.
---

Text-size sliders belong on a dedicated screen opened from Settings, not inline
in the Settings list. The Settings How to Use destination should extend the
onboarding guide with the guarded-adjustment explanations; do not keep a
separate Guarded Adjustments Settings entry or page.

**Why:** the user requested that layout and the consolidation of the guard
explanations into How to Use.

**How to apply:** preserve the existing scale behavior and guard-owner flows
while changing where their controls and explanations are presented.
