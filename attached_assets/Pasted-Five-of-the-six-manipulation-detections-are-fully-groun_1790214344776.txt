Five of six manipulation detections shipped; here's the honest accounting of what happened with the sixth.

**Shipped, fully grounded in existing schema:**

- **Variable Reward Loop** and **Infinite Session Design** share a two-pass design — cheap aggregate filtering from `getSessionStatsByDay` first, then raw session fetch only for the small shortlist that survives. This matters because computing a true coefficient of variation needs raw durations (variance doesn't aggregate linearly across days), but fetching raw sessions for every app the user has would be wasteful.
- **Morning Hijack** is per-candidate-package now (matching the original spec more precisely than my first pass at it) — for each app that was ever first-opened, what fraction of all days it held that spot. This one also wires into the clarifying-question system from IMPL_1B: if the qualifying days average a 7+ rating, it queues a question instead of assuming the pattern is a problem.
- **Escalating Capture** and **Streak Lock-in** both work directly off `daily_app_usage` — no new queries needed beyond `getForDateRange`.

**A design decision worth knowing about:** each detector returns at most one finding, not one per qualifying app. This isn't simplification for its own sake — `FindingRepository`'s cooldown logic silently drops any finding submitted while the 7-day surface limit is active. Submitting three qualifying apps in one run would lose two of them outright. Returning only the strongest match means nothing is wasted; a runner-up that still qualifies gets evaluated fresh next time.
